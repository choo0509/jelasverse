How It Works

Support that changes with the learner.

Five stages. One principle: help learners become more independent over time — not more dependent on the tool.

01
BELAJAR
Understand one idea at a time

Focused material without unnecessary information. Each concept stands clearly on its own, reducing cognitive load for readers who struggle with dense text.

Focused
02
SAYA TAK FAHAM
Get a different route when stuck

The same concept changes representation — a paragraph becomes a concept map, a list becomes a timeline, a group becomes a relationship diagram.

Transforms
03
CUBA
Actively work with the idea

Place, connect, select or reconstruct — not just read. Active interaction strengthens understanding more effectively than re-reading.

Active
04
INGAT
Rebuild it from memory later

Return to the same idea with information gradually removed. Retrieval practice — not re-reading — builds durable, lasting memory.

Retrieval
05
KURANG BANTUAN
Need less support over time

As the learner succeeds independently, unnecessary support reduces. The goal is growing independence — not more help.

Independence

Adaptive Representations

History isn't one kind of information. Why should every lesson look the same?

Different historical ideas have different structures. JelasVerse changes the representation to match the structure of the idea.

JelasVerse does not assume every dyslexic learner is a "visual learner." The representation changes because different historical ideas have different structures. No learning style labels. No diagnosis.
INGAT — Retrieval Practice

Don't just read it again. Rebuild it.

Research shows that retrieving information from memory is far more effective than re-reading. INGAT makes this systematic — gradually removing scaffolding until learners reconstruct from memory alone.

"The goal of support is not dependence. It is independence."
